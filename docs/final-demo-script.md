# Final Demo Script

## Demo Duration

**Recommended duration:** 5–7 minutes

---

## 1. Opening

**Project Title:** RSU Nexus – Campus Resource Marketplace

**Problem**

RSU students often struggle to find equipment, textbooks, tutors, and campus services because information is scattered across class chat groups, social media, and personal contacts.

**Target Users**

- RSU students
- Student organizations
- Tutors
- Campus service providers

**Value Proposition**

RSU Nexus provides one centralized marketplace where students can discover, borrow, rent, share, offer resources, and hire student services quickly and securely.

---

## 2. User Scenario

Imagine a student who needs a DSLR camera for a multimedia assignment. Instead of asking in multiple class chat groups, the student visits RSU Nexus, explores the marketplace, searches for a camera, submits a borrowing request, communicates with the owner through the built-in messaging feature, and tracks the request status from the dashboard.

## 4. Evidence and Validation

The prototype was evaluated through user testing with RSU students.

Testing demonstrated that users were able to:

- Successfully explore marketplace listings.
- Search and filter resources.
- View listing details.
- Submit borrowing and rental requests.
- Communicate with resource owners.
- Track request status using the dashboard.

Feedback indicated that the workflow is easy to understand, while recommending minor improvements to labels, navigation, and interface consistency. These improvements have been documented in the Final Improvement List.

---

## 5. Closing

RSU Nexus simplifies how students discover, borrow, rent, share, and offer campus resources and services through one trusted marketplace.

By replacing scattered communication channels with a centralized platform, RSU Nexus helps students save time, access resources more efficiently, and collaborate within the university community.

The project has completed implementation, documentation, validation, and user testing, and is ready for final refinement before the Lab 14 MVP presentation.
