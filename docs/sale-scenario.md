# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

**RSU Nexus – Campus Resource Marketplace**

---

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary user:**
  RSU students who need to borrow, rent, share, or offer equipment, learning resources, tutoring, and student services.

- **Secondary user:**
  Student clubs, tutors, campus organizations, and students who own resources or provide services.

- **Decision-maker or admin role, if any:**
  Marketplace administrator who reviews listings, manages reported content, and monitors platform activity.

---

## 3. Situation / Context

A second-year ICT student has a multimedia assignment due next week and needs a DSLR camera. Buying one is too expensive, and asking classmates individually through chat groups is slow and unreliable. The student also needs to find someone offering video editing assistance. They visit RSU Nexus to search available equipment and services, compare listings, and submit a request through one platform.

---

## 4. Pain Point

Students often spend too much time searching through class chats, social media, and personal contacts to find equipment, tutors, textbooks, or student services. Information is scattered, availability is unclear, and many opportunities are missed because there is no centralized campus marketplace.

---

## 5. Product Introduction

A classmate recommends RSU Nexus after successfully borrowing a calculator for an exam. The student opens the landing page, explores the marketplace, searches for a DSLR camera, views available listings, submits a request, and tracks the request status from a single platform.

---

## 6. Value Message

**For RSU students who experience difficulty finding equipment, learning resources, tutors, or student services, our RSU Nexus Campus Resource Marketplace helps them quickly discover, borrow, rent, share, and offer campus resources through one centralized marketplace with an easy search and request workflow.**

---

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

"Are you still asking around in chat groups whenever you need a camera, calculator, tutor, textbook, or student service? RSU Nexus brings everything together in one campus marketplace. You can search available resources, connect with verified students, submit requests, and track their status—all from one easy-to-use platform. Instead of wasting time searching, you can quickly find what you need and focus on your studies."

---

## 8. Expected User Action

After seeing the product, the user should:

- Explore the marketplace
- Search for equipment, learning resources, tutors, or services
- View listing details
- Submit a borrowing, rental, or service request
- Create a listing to share or offer resources
- Give feedback about the platform
- Recommend RSU Nexus to other students

---
