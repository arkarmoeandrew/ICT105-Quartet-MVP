# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

**RSU Nexus – Campus Resource Marketplace**

---

## Target Early Users

The first target users are Rangsit University students who want to borrow, rent, share, exchange resources, or hire student skills and services through one trusted campus marketplace. Early testing will focus on active students, student clubs, and classmates who frequently use or provide campus resources.

---

## Main Value Proposition

RSU Nexus provides one trusted marketplace where RSU students can discover, borrow, rent, share, and offer campus resources and services quickly and safely.

---

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
|---------|-------------|---------------|----------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Class chat groups | RSU Students | Discover, rent, borrow, or offer campus resources in one place | Click prototype link | Link clicks / Demo views | Students actively communicate through class groups |
| Campus QR Posters | Students on campus | Instantly explore equipment, learning resources, and student services | Scan QR Code | QR scans / Landing page visits | Students pass these locations every day |
| Instagram Story / Post | RSU Student Community | A modern campus marketplace for resources and student services | Visit landing page | Story clicks / Profile visits | Instagram is widely used by university students |

---

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare the landing page and prototype demonstration.
2. Share the landing page through selected student communication channels.
3. Monitor landing page visits and prototype usage.
4. Collect user feedback through Google Forms and interviews.
5. Improve the prototype, marketing message, and user experience based on feedback.

---

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Students may not immediately understand all available marketplace features. | Clearly explain borrowing, renting, sharing, and hiring services on the landing page with icons and examples. |
| Low student engagement during early testing. | Promote the prototype through multiple channels including class groups, Instagram, and campus QR posters. |
| Users may encounter prototype bugs or incomplete features. | Test all core workflows before demonstrations and quickly resolve reported issues. |
| Students may hesitate to share personal information. | Only collect essential information and clearly explain the privacy policy and secure authentication. |
| Low feedback participation. | Use a short Google Form and encourage classmates to provide feedback during demonstrations. |
