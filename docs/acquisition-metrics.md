# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose
Measure early user interest, landing page performance, and engagement with the RSU Nexus prototype to evaluate the effectiveness of the landing page and go-to-market strategy.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|---------------------------|-------------|----------------------------|
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Number of users who visit the landing page | Count total page visits | Landing page analytics or spreadsheet | At least **100 landing page views** |
| M-02 | CTA Clicks | Number of users who click the **Explore Marketplace** or **Create Listing** button | CTA clicks ÷ Landing page views × 100 | JavaScript click counter / manual log | **35% or higher** click-through rate |
| M-03 | Prototype Demo Attempts | Number of users who open or test the RSU Nexus prototype | Count visits through prototype link or QR code | Prototype link, QR scans, manual log | At least **50 demo attempts** |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Number of users who provide feedback after testing | Count submitted feedback forms | Google Form or feedback spreadsheet | At least **20 feedback responses** |
| M-05 | Interest Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors who perform a target action (demo, feedback, or testing) | (Demo Attempts + Feedback Responses) ÷ Landing Page Views × 100 | Spreadsheet or analytics report | **30% or higher** conversion rate |

## Interpretation

If the metrics do not meet the target, the group will:

- Improve the landing page headline and value proposition.
- Make the CTA buttons more visible and persuasive.
- Simplify navigation from the landing page to the prototype.
- Improve promotional messages on class chats, social media, and QR posters.
- Add clearer screenshots and feature explanations.
- Collect additional feedback from RSU students and refine the prototype based on their suggestions.
- Continue monitoring acquisition metrics after each update to measure improvement.
